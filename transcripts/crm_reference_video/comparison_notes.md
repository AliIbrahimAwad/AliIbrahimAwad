# Reference CRM Vs Internal CRM

## Data Model

- Reference CRM is centered on a lead/deal pipeline.
- Internal CRM is moving toward contact ownership as the source of truth.

## Routing

- Reference CRM appears to use basic round-robin or similarly simple assignment.
- Internal CRM uses availability-aware, eligibility-based routing with contact continuity.

## Automation

- Reference CRM relies on rule-based triggers and follow-up logic.
- Internal CRM is positioned for AI-assisted automation, including transcription and status support.

## Product Focus

- Reference CRM emphasizes UI clarity and visible workflow.
- Internal CRM emphasizes backend integrity, deduplication, ownership continuity, and data reliability.

## Communication Layer

- Reference CRM treats calling, texting, and email as embedded operator tools.
- Internal CRM also treats communication records as analyzable data for AI and timeline workflows.

## Inventory

- Reference CRM appears to link inventory into deals and pipeline views.
- Internal CRM already performs active inventory ingestion and can evolve toward smarter matching and workflow ties.

## Manager Control

- Reference CRM focuses on visibility into distribution and results.
- Internal CRM adds direct operational control over routing, rep availability, and ownership logic.

## Current Internal Gaps Relative To Reference

- No advanced visual pipeline board yet
- No deep reporting dashboards yet
- No deployed AI agents yet
- Contacts/ownership model is stronger internally, but the UI still needs more polish to surface that strength
