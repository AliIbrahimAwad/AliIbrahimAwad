# CRM Reference Video Key Features

## High-Level Shape

- Dealership CRM with a pipeline-first workflow
- Visual stage-based management from new lead through sold/lost
- Manager-facing dashboard for team activity and performance

## Navigation

- Sidebar-based navigation
- Separate areas for leads, deals, contacts, and reporting

## Lead Intake

- Multiple lead sources
- Website form submissions
- AutoTrader
- Other marketplace-style sources such as CarGurus-equivalent flows

## Lead Workflow

- New leads enter the pipeline
- Leads are assigned to a rep manually or with basic automation
- Reps can take immediate actions from the CRM
- Calls
- SMS
- Email
- Leads move across visible pipeline stages

## Automation

- Rule-based triggers
- Follow-up reminders or simple workflow automation
- No indication of advanced AI-driven orchestration in the reference notes

## Manager Functions

- Team performance visibility
- Lead distribution visibility
- Conversion and activity reporting

## Inventory

- Vehicle records are linked into the sales workflow
- Inventory appears connected to deals rather than acting as a passive spreadsheet only

## Communications

- Built-in calling and texting
- Communication tools appear embedded in the CRM workflow rather than external-only
